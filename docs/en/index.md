# IdentityOps

> A research experiment: a small language for specifying, executing, and auditing
> identity-relevant operations under declared criteria.

IdentityOps is not a merge engine and makes no claim of novelty. It asks whether one
small language can express the commitments an existing system makes about sameness,
compare them with a declared criterion, and run proposed transformations against a
model state, in three different settings.
